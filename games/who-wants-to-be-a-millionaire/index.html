GAME RULES & HOW TO PLAY

💰 THE 15-STEP MONEY LADDER

Answer multiple-choice English grammar questions to climb from 100 points up to 25,000 points. Difficulty increases at every stage (Easy ➔ Medium ➔ Hard ➔ Very Hard)!

⚠️ THE RESET RULE (NO ELIMINATIONS)

If your team answers incorrectly, you are NOT eliminated. Instead, your team's current ladder progress resets to 0 points and the turn passes to the next team. You can climb back up on your next turn!

🃏 3 TEAM JOKERS (ONE USE EACH)

  • 👨‍🏫 Ask the Teacher: Ask your teacher for a grammar hint or explanation.
  • ✂️ 50:50: Removes two incorrect options, leaving only the correct answer and 1 wrong answer.
  • 🔁 Double Answer: Gives your team 2 chances on this question. If your first pick is wrong, you get to choose a second option!

⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

1, 2, 3, 4 or A, B, C, D: Select answer options • Space / Enter: Confirm / Next question • M: Toggle Sound
