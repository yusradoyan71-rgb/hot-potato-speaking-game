🎡 HOW TO PLAY ENGLISH WHEEL

1
Spin the Wheel: Land on point values (100–1000) or special wedges like DOUBLE, BONUS, PASS, or BANKRUPT.
2
Guess Consonants: Choose a consonant. If correct, you earn points for every occurrence and keep your turn!
3
Buy Vowels: If your team has at least 500 round points, you can buy a vowel (A, E, I, O, U) to reveal vowels in the puzzle.
4
Solve the Puzzle: When you know the expression, press SOLVE to type it in and win the round bonus!
5
Final Round: The highest-scoring team advances to the Final Round for a chance at +3,000 BONUS POINTS!
