HOW TO PLAY

1. Read the Text Clue

Every word features an English category, definition, or missing sentence context. No picture clues!

2. Guess Letters

Click the on-screen letters or use your physical keyboard to reveal blanks. Correct letters appear in the word.

3. Watch the Hangman Figure

Each incorrect guess adds one body part to the figure (Head, Torso, Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg, Right Leg). You have 6 mistakes before the round is over!

4. Build Streaks & Beat the Clock

Solve consecutive words to trigger Streak Multipliers. In timed levels, solve quickly for extra bonus points!
