🏝️ How to Play Escape the Island

Story & Objective

Your team is stranded on an uncharted island. To escape, complete 6 survival missions by answering English grammar and vocabulary challenges.

Missions & Items

  • Mission 1 (🌴 Jungle): Find Compass & Map
  • Mission 2 (🗿 Temple): Recover Boat Key
  • Mission 3 (🛖 Camp): Secure Battery Pack
  • Mission 4 (📻 Radio): Tune Long-Range Radio
  • Mission 5 (🚤 Boat): Collect Emergency Fuel
  • Mission 6 (🚁 Rescue): Broadcast SOS coordinates and escape!

Energy / Lives (❤️❤️❤️)

Each incorrect answer costs 1 energy heart. If you lose all 3 hearts, you can rest and retry the mission!
