📖 HOW TO PLAY & SHORTCUTS

🎮 Core Rules

  • Pass the Bomb: Teams take turns receiving the bomb in fixed rotation.
  • Pick Speaker: The team chooses which teammate will answer.
  • 15-Second Timer: Student speaks aloud to answer before time runs out!
  • Teacher Judgement: Teacher presses ANSWERED (+1 pt) or BOOM (0 pts).
  • No Elimination: Everyone stays in the game for maximum fun and practice!
  • Chaos Rounds: Every 5 rounds brings funny voice/action challenges (+2 pts)!

⌨️ Teacher Keyboard Shortcuts

Space or A Mark Answered (+1/+2 pts)
B or X Trigger BOOM (0 pts, pass bomb)
S Skip Question (same student, timer resets)
P Pause / Resume Game
M Toggle Sound ON / OFF
1–6 Quick-select speaker 1 to 6
